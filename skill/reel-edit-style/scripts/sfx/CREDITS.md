# Sound credits

Every sound here can be shared and used in commercial videos.

- **Code-made sounds** (whoosh, swoosh, impact, pop, ding, riser, and in `extra/`: whip, thump, swell, drop, glitch, snap, chime, boom, riser_long). These come from the earshot sound bank (MIT License, (c) 2026 Leon Lin, https://github.com/Leonxlnx/earshot). They are synthesized, not recorded.
- **CC0 recordings** (click, tick, and `extra/key`), via the earshot bank:
  - click: "Plastic Clicks" by qubodup (freesound.org/s/167829, CC0 1.0)
  - tick: "fit/tick/domino" (CC0 1.0)
  - key: "fit/key/thock" (CC0 1.0)
- **Fallback kit:** `scripts/sfx2.py` generates its own original sounds if this folder is missing.

To use your own sounds (for example Mixkit downloads), put them in your project's `sfx/` folder named whoosh, swoosh, impact, pop, click, ding, riser or tick (`.wav`). Don't redistribute Mixkit files themselves; their license allows using them in videos, not sharing the files.
